# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information, or sustained disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
